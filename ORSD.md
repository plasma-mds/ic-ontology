|**Domain**|Liquid sample diagnostic using ion chromatography|
|**Conceptualized by**|Robert Wagner|
|**Implemented by**|Robert Wagner \& Ihda Chaerony Siffa|
|**Purpose**|Detailed description of ion chromatography measurements (with the focus on ion-exchange chromatography) for enabling easier method adoption and measurement comparison.|
|**Level of formality**|Semi-informal|
|**Scope**|Terms needed to formally describe the measurement and device settings for electrically suppressed, chemically suppressed and unsuppressed anion and cation chromatography measurements and the used processing methods (if applicable).|
